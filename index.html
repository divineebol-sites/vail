    Common questions

    Who is the developer?

    SM Development Corporation (SMDC). Property management is by SMDC's property management team, with SMDC Leasing available if you want help renting out your unit.

    What is the ceiling height?

    2.80 m in the living areas, and 2.40 m in the kitchen and toilet and bath, per SMDC's project brief.

    What comes with the unit?

    Ceramic tile floors, painted walls, laminated wood main and bedroom doors, aluminum casement windows, and a water closet, lavatory and shower set in the toilet and bath.

    Who provides the utilities?

    Power from CEPALCO and water from the Cagayan de Oro Water District, per the project brief.

    Is there parking?

    Yes. The residential parking ratio is 1 slot for every 6 units, so parking is limited. Ask me early if you need a slot.

    Can I reserve even if I'm not in CDO?

    Yes. Reservations are accepted in Cagayan de Oro, Davao and Manila, and I can walk you through everything online if you're abroad.

    Where is the showroom?

    3rd Floor, SM City CDO Uptown, a short walk from the site.
